# Novo

> Novo is a New York- and Miami-based financial technology company (founded 2016) operating a digital business-banking platform for small businesses, entrepreneurs, and freelancers. Novo is not a chartered bank; it is a service provider to Middlesex Federal Savings, F.A. (Member FDIC), which holds deposits and provides the underlying banking products. Novo publishes no first-party public developer API and runs no developer portal; consumer-permissioned account data (Auth, Balance, Identity, Transactions) is reached by third parties only through the Plaid aggregator.
